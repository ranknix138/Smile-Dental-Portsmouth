Recovery Guidance

Instructions After a Dental Extraction

Follow these steps during the first hours and days after an extraction to help your mouth heal comfortably.

1. Rest

Rest for a few hours following treatment and avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours.

2. Gentle Rinsing

Do not rinse on day one. From tomorrow, rinse gently with 1 tsp salt in warm water every 4 hours for 4 days.

3. Food & Drink

Avoid hot drinks while numb, and avoid alcohol, smoking or hard foods for 24 hours.

4. Bleeding Control

Sit upright and bite firmly on damp gauze for 15 minutes if bleeding resumes.

5. Pain Relief

Take paracetamol or ibuprofen if safe for you. Avoid aspirin as it affects clotting.

6. When to Call Us

Call 02393 234105 if bleeding persists or pain worsens. Out of hours, call NHS 111.
